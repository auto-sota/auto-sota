# Auto Sota updates

What has been added to [autosota.com](https://autosota.com), newest first. New catalogs go in every day; this list keeps the bigger changes.

## Coming next

- New languages: French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic
- Car diagrams: the car maker's exploded-view drawings for your car, with the parts list for each

## November 2026

- **Car diagrams.** The car maker's own exploded-view drawings, with every part you can buy numbered.
- **Volkswagen group and Renault factory catalogs.** With factory parts drawings and VIN search.
- **List look-up.** Paste a whole list of part numbers and get every brand back as one price sheet.
- **Cross reference page.** How dealer, aftermarket and superseded numbers relate, with worked examples from the catalog.
- **Spark plug cross reference chart.** NGK, Denso, Bosch, Champion and Beru plugs matched to each other by thread, reach, hex and seat.
- **VIN decoder pages by make.** Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi and Subaru: what each VIN position means and where to find the VIN on the car.
- **Six new guides** on cross referencing, VIN look-up and reading part numbers.

## October 2026

- **Check my car.** Save your car once and every part page tells you if the part fits it.
- **More cars.** VW Group factory catalog, plus Chery, BYD, Geely and Renault.
- **More brands.** Brembo, Akebono and Fel-Pro catalogs, with replaced part numbers.
- **More to look at.** 360° views, fitting videos, maker diagrams and car photos on brand parts.
- **Part types by car.** Pick a car and a type of part and see every number for it.
- **Saved parts and recently viewed parts.**
- **Prices in more currencies**, refreshed every week.
- Logos for many more aftermarket brands.
